After saving, the device joins that network. Reconnect your phone to the same network and open .local or the address shown above.

Connection mode

Switching restarts the device. Matter mode is paired with your Matter app over Bluetooth and the app chooses Wi-Fi or Thread. If it ends up on Thread this page is no longer reachable; to come back to Wi-Fi mode type mode wifi on the USB console (keeps the pairing), or hold the BOOT button for 10 seconds and let go (factory reset, erases the pairing too). Switching modes keeps the pairing.

Options

Bypass: never send commands (relay only)
Extended telemetry probing (experimental)
Extended telemetry sends the vendor tool's handshake every minute to unlock extra readings (voltage, flame temperature…). It can upset the controller on some units; watch for “stale” after switching it on.
